# Sport Lifestyle Passport Spec

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Passport represents verified lifestyle reputation
The product SHALL provide a Sport Lifestyle Passport that summarizes a user's sport lifestyle identity, proof history, badges, challenge progress, rank, and matched opportunity signals.

#### Scenario: User views passport
- **WHEN** a user opens their Sport Lifestyle Passport
- **THEN** they see selected sport lifestyle interests, earned badges, active challenges, verification history, rank status, and relevant opportunity matches.

### Requirement: Passport is behavior-first
The Sport Lifestyle Passport SHALL prioritize repeated lifestyle behavior and proof over generic points, generic rewards, or voucher collection.

#### Scenario: User has repeated proofs
- **WHEN** a user repeatedly submits valid or simulated sport lifestyle proof
- **THEN** the passport emphasizes consistency, badge tier progression, challenge completion, and ranking impact
- **AND** does not describe progress primarily as loyalty points or voucher eligibility.

### Requirement: Passport supports career-worthy signal
The Sport Lifestyle Passport SHALL be structured so high-quality lifestyle activity can become a signal for ambassador, sponsorship, event, coaching, creator, or career opportunities in future phases.

#### Scenario: User reaches high-signal status
- **WHEN** a user earns Diamond or Elite tier badges and maintains consistent challenge completion
- **THEN** the passport can present that activity as high-signal lifestyle reputation
- **AND** can support future opportunity matching beyond discounts.

### Requirement: Web2-first public language
The Sport Lifestyle Passport SHALL use consumer-friendly sport lifestyle language and avoid crypto-first or Web3-heavy terminology in primary user-facing copy.

#### Scenario: User reads passport copy
- **WHEN** passport sections describe badges, proofs, ranking, or opportunities
- **THEN** copy uses plain-language concepts such as verified lifestyle, proof, rank, challenge, and opportunity
- **AND** avoids making wallets, chains, or tokens the primary framing.
